# Multi-model AI video/image aggregators and pay-per-use API marketplaces: value for money (state as of 17 November 2026)

**Method and limits (applies to every section, read first).**
- Research date: 17 Nov 2026. 22 web searches completed, most of them restricted to the vendor's own domain so that the result text came from vendor pricing pages, help centers and docs.
- Direct page fetches were blocked in this session (hosts such as magnific.com, higgsfield.ai, krea.ai, openart.ai and fal.ai are outside the session's network allowlist). Every figure below is therefore "as reported in the search-result summary of the cited page", not a full-page read. Where one bullet lists several links, the search tool summarized them together and the exact page could not be pinned down.
- The shared web-search budget for the session ran out before 8 further queries could run. Not researched at all: Trustpilot/Reddit/press complaints, Together AI, minimum top-ups at fal/Replicate/Runware/WaveSpeed, Magnific per-video credit costs, Leonardo per-video token costs, most refund/cancellation terms, regional (EU/UK) limits, dedicated "agent" video makers. These are listed under Gaps and must not be filled from memory.
- Source labels used below: [V] vendor pricing page/help/docs; [VB] vendor blog or SEO page (marketing); [C] competitor-authored content; [3P] third-party blog or comparison site (often affiliate-driven, treat with caution); [P] press.
- Page ages are given where the search tool reported them (for example "about 11 days old" = roughly 6 Nov 2026).
- Model landscape visible on these platforms in Nov 2026 (names only; capabilities are covered by other researchers): Seedance 2.5 and 2.0 (ByteDance), Kling 4.0, 3.0 and O3 (Kuaishou), Veo 3.1 in Lite/Fast/standard tiers (Google), Wan 3.0 (Alibaba), MiniMax H3 and Hailuo 2.3, Runway Gen-4.5 and Aleph 2.0, LTX-2.3/2.5, Grok Imagine Video 1.5. OpenAI's Sora 2 API is reported as retired on 24 Sep 2026, so Sora entries in credit tables are probably stale — [ugccopilot price index, Oct 2026, 3P](https://ugccopilot.ai/research/ai-video-model-price-index/); [invideo blog, Aug 2026, VB](https://invideo.io/blog/ai-video-model-pricing/); [dev.to/skilaai, 3P](https://dev.to/skilaai/google-just-made-ai-video-50-cheaper-openai-killed-sora-heres-the-new-pricing-math-1ag4). Not verified against OpenAI.

## 1. For each aggregator: models offered, plan prices (monthly/annual), credits per plan, credit cost per clip, effective USD per 5 s and 10 s clip

### Takeaway
Entry plans cluster at $9-20/month and mid plans at $30-60/month, but the effective price of one clip from a current top model differs by more than 5x between platforms: a 5 s Kling 3.0 Pro 1080p clip works out to about $0.26-0.61 on Artlist but $0.94-1.30 on ImagineArt, and Seedance 2.5 or Veo 3.1 (standard) clips cost roughly $0.84-$4 each everywhere. Freepik is now called Magnific (since 28 Apr 2026), and several vendors (Higgsfield, OpenArt, Magnific, invideo, Hedra) show two or three different price sets within weeks, so every number needs a live check before purchase.

## 2. "Unlimited" offers: which platforms, which models, which plans, and the fine print

### Takeaway
No platform found offers open-ended unlimited generation on a current flagship video model: unlimited video is either restricted to older or lower-resolution models in a relaxed queue (Leonardo Ultimate, Artlist yearly Core/Creator, ImagineArt Ultimate), or limited to 7-day windows and paid passes (Higgsfield), or is mostly an image perk that is being narrowed (Magnific, OpenArt). Envato's earlier unlimited video promotions ended in early 2026 and were replaced by credits.

- **Magnific**: Plus, Business and Pro plans include unlimited generations for selected AI models; unlimited applies only in the Magnific web app (API calls always use credits); on Plus and Business "several models are moving from Unlimited to credits", effective at each subscriber's next renewal — [Magnific help: unlimited models, V](https://www.magnific.com/ai/docs/unlimited-models)

## 3. Credit mechanics and traps: expiry, rollover, top-ups, annual-only discounts, renewal, refunds, complaints

### Takeaway
Most credits are use-it-or-lose-it each month (Lovart, ImagineArt even on annual plans), with rollover only in specific cases (Leonardo's token bank, Figma Weave Professional, OpenArt add-on credits), and the best per-clip prices usually require annual prepayment. The complaint record (Trustpilot, Reddit, press) could not be researched in this session; the only signals found are indirect.

### Cited Findings
- **Leonardo**: rollover bank of up to 75,000 tokens on Premium and 180,000 on Ultimate — [Leonardo pricing, V](https://leonardo.ai/pricing)
- **OpenArt**: add-on credits are sold in 5,000-credit increments; unused add-on credits (the part above monthly plan credits) roll over to the next month — [OpenArt help center, V](https://openart.ai/help/)
- **Lovart**: monthly-plan credits refresh on the billing date and leftovers are cleared; top-up packs bought after 30 Jun 2026 expire 366 days after purchase — [Lovart pricing FAQ, V](https://www.lovart.ai/pricing); [aireiter, 3P](https://aireiter.com/blog/lovart-pricing)
- **ImagineArt**: credits refresh monthly even on annual plans; yearly billing is about 30% cheaper — [ImagineArt subscription plans, V](https://docs.imagine.art/account/subscription-plans)
- **Figma Weave**: Professional includes credit rollover; Starter allows top-ups — [wireflow, 3P](https://www.wireflow.ai/blog/figma-weave-pricing)

- **Higgsfield**: Starter has no annual discount; prices vary by country and exclude tax; three different price sets appear on its own pages (see section 1) — [Krea blog on Higgsfield pricing, C](https://www.krea.ai/blog/higgsfield-pricing-explained-2026-unlimited-credits-and-real-monthly-costs); [Higgsfield pricing guide, VB](https://geo.higgsfield.ai/higgsfield-ai-pricing-guide)

**Overall reading for the stated user (solo, weak PC, 3D-style social clips, lowest reasonable cost, fast)**
- Everything here is cloud-based, so the weak PC is not a constraint.
- At 20-60 clips a month the workload is small enough that pay-per-use beats most subscriptions on monthly billing; subscriptions become worthwhile only with an annual commitment or for their tooling (editor, stock, director modes).
- Speed favors platforms with parallel generation (OpenArt Pro lists 32 parallel jobs) and disfavors relaxed "unlimited" queues.
- Avoid annual prepayment for a perk that the vendor can narrow (Magnific's unlimited list, OpenArt's promotional unlimited, Higgsfield's windows).
