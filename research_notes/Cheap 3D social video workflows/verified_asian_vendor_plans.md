## 6. Terms of service: licence, training and prohibited content

Each puts responsibility for uploaded faces, logos and characters on the user and bans content that infringes likeness, copyright or trademark rights.

- On neutral blind-vote evidence, the strongest value for a stylised-3D creator who starts from a finished still is **MiniMax H3 at 768p** (4th in image-to-video, about $0.37 per 5-second clip on Hailuo Standard) and, for best quality regardless of cost, **Seedance 2.5** (1st in image-to-video, about $1.76 per 5-second clip). The cheap Seedance 2.0 Mini and Hailuo 2.3-Fast tiers are not ranked, so their quality for 3D looks is unknown.
- Kling's reputation for a glossy "3D CGI" look and the claymation win in the one independent stylised test point to Kling as a reasonable choice for toy and clay looks, but both observations concern Kling 3.0 or a single reviewer; Kling 4.0 has no independent ranking yet and its 1080p is not live.
- Wan 3.0 leads text-to-video but drops to 11th when animating a supplied image, and reviewers report multi-reference drift and unrequested cuts — a weaker fit for a recurring mascot than its headline rank suggests. Vidu's low arena ranks (20th for Q4 Preview, 30th and 33rd for Q3) sit oddly with its marketing as a consistency specialist; arena votes do not measure cross-clip consistency.
- For ordinary users the disputes have changed what the tools will accept, not whether they can be used: uploads of real faces and prompts for known characters are filtered on Seedance, outputs carry provenance marks, and Hailuo's US litigation is unresolved. An adverse ruling or settlement could change Hailuo's filters or availability at short notice (speculative), which is an argument for monthly rather than annual billing there.
- EU creators are covered by Dreamina's price schedules in most EEA states and the UK, should expect local-currency prices plus VAT rather than the US figures in these notes, and since 2 Aug 2026 should expect AI-generated video to be machine-marked by the provider; purely stylised animation that does not depict real people or events is unlikely to be a "deep fake" requiring a visible label, but that reading is mine and not legal advice.

### Gaps
